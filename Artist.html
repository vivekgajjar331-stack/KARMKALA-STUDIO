Founder & Artist

MANTHAN PATEL

Art is more than just drawing or designing. It is a way of expressing emotions, stories and personality.

THE ARTIST BEHIND KARMAKALA I am a young, self-taught artist from Rajkot, driven by curiosity, imagination and the freedom to create without limits. I never wanted to be defined by just one form of art. From tattooing skin to painting on canvas, designing clothes and creating bleach art, I believe every surface has its own story waiting to be told. Karmakala Studio is more than a studio to me. It is a space where ideas become art, experiments become creations, and every piece carries a part of my vision. No boundaries. No fixed medium. Just art.
